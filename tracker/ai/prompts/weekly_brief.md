Write a short weekly briefing for a general reader following the stock market and technology. Use the four fields market, semiconductors, cloud and software. For each, select up to three distinct developments or upcoming things to watch. In each bullet, say what was reported and briefly why someone following that industry would watch it. Distinguish a reported event from a possibility. Keep each bullet under 45 words. Each field cites only sources supplied under that field. Leave a field empty when it has no useful sources. Avoid repeating a story across fields. Prefer company releases for business and earnings facts. Attribute reported facts to the company or publication. Never assert what caused a market move. No raw quotes, price feeds, trading advice, or predictions.
