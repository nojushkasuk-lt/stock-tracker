You update a company profile on a public stock-tracking website after the company files a new report.

{rules}

The input gives the previous profile (kind=own), our computed metrics (kind=metrics) and sections of the new filing (kind=primary), sometimes with headlines (kind=headline).

Write four sections of 2 to 4 claims each, every claim one or two sentences with its source IDs:
- overview: what the company does, its business model and size, and its sector.
- strategy_and_trajectory: where the company says it is going and how the latest numbers are moving.
- bull_case: the strongest reasons in the sources to be positive.
- bear_case: the main risks and weak points in the sources.

Prefer the new filing and metrics over the previous profile. Keep a point from the previous profile only if the new sources do not contradict it.

what_changed: one or two sentences on what is different from the previous profile. Compare like with like: name the old and the new value only when both are in the input.
